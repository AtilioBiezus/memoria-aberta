Memória Aberta – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Memória Aberta is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels ("Memória Aberta", "Cosmo Doodles Space" and "The Stories Jesus Told") on YouTube and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and no other person signs in to it.

YouTube (Google) data

Data we access

  • Upload and manage videos on the owner's channel (youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl): uploading and scheduling the channel's own videos, setting titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reading and replying to comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel (yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time and traffic sources, used only by the owner to decide what to produce next.

How data is used and stored

  • The authorization token and any report data are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are sent only to Google's own servers.
  • No data from other YouTube users is collected or stored, except the text of public comments on the owner's own videos, read so the owner can reply.
  • Memória Aberta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted.

TikTok data

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm that the correct account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How data is stored

  • The authorization token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is never sent to any server other than TikTok's.
  • No data from other TikTok users is collected, stored or processed.

Deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted.

Sharing

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
