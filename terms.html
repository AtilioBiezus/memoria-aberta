Memória Aberta – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Memória Aberta is a private publishing tool used only by its owner to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels ("Memória Aberta", "Cosmo Doodles Space" and "The Stories Jesus Told") on YouTube and TikTok.

  1. Who can use it. The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in, with Google (YouTube) or TikTok, using the channels' own accounts.
  2. What it does. After the owner authorizes it, the tool uploads and schedules videos produced by the channels on YouTube, reads the channels' own analytics and comments so the owner can reply, and sends videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts. Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it goes live.
  3. Content. All uploaded content is original material produced by the channels. AI-generated elements are disclosed as YouTube's and TikTok's rules require. The owner is responsible for complying with the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and both platforms' Community Guidelines.
  4. Revoking access. Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.
  5. No warranty. The tool is provided as is, for the owner's own use.
