Last updated: November 9, 2026

Memória Aberta is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the "Memória Aberta" channel to send the channel's own videos to its TikTok account as drafts.

  1. Who can use it. The tool is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner logs in with TikTok, using the channel's own account.

  3. Content. All uploaded content is original material produced by the channel. AI-generated elements are disclosed as TikTok's rules require. The owner is responsible for complying with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  4. Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.
  5. No warranty. The tool is provided as is, for the owner's own use.
